5 Golden Rules for Birthday Gifting

  1. Personalization beats price. A $30 item with her name on it beats a $100 generic candle set every time.
  2. Think about her daily life. Something she'll use every morning (a mug, a tote, a necklace) keeps giving throughout the year.
  3. Digital gifts are valid. Especially for last-minute — a beautifully designed printable card or bundle can be as touching as a physical gift.
  4. Pair small gifts together. Two $25 items presented together feel more generous than one $40 item.
  5. Add a handwritten note. Always. No exceptions.
