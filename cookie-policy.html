Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use on cadomalo.com, and how you can control them. It is part of our broader Privacy Policy. By continuing to use our site after accepting cookies, you agree to our use of cookies as described here.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit. They allow the site to remember your actions and preferences over time, so you do not have to re-enter information every time you return or navigate between pages.

Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies: Temporary files that are deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies: Files that remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them
  • First-party cookies: Set directly by cadomalo.com
  • Third-party cookies: Set by external services we use (analytics, advertising, etc.)

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep your shopping cart active as you browse
  • Keep you logged in to your account
  • Remember your cookie preferences
  • Understand how people use our site so we can improve it
  • Show you relevant ads on other platforms after you visit us
  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies No consent required

These cookies are essential for cadomalo.com to function properly. Without them, core features like your shopping cart, checkout, and account login would not work. We do not need your consent to use these cookies under GDPR because they are technically required.

3.2 Analytics Cookies Consent required

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website — which pages are most popular, how long visitors stay, where they come from, and what they click on. This information is used in aggregate to improve our site. We only activate these cookies if you consent.

Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymized data about site usage. GA4 tracks:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • How you arrived at our site (search engine, direct link, social media, etc.)
  • Your general geographic region (country/city level, not precise location)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • Which products were viewed, added to cart, or purchased (anonymized event data)

Google Analytics uses IP anonymization by default in GA4. Data collected is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
_ga Distinguishes unique users for Google Analytics reporting 2 years
_ga_[ID] Maintains GA4 session state and stores session data 2 years
_gid Distinguishes users — used to throttle request rate 24 hours

3.3 Marketing and Advertising Cookies Consent required

Marketing cookies enable us to show you relevant ads on other platforms after you visit cadomalo.com, and to measure how effective those ads are. These cookies track your browsing activity across different websites. We only activate these cookies if you consent.

Facebook Pixel (Meta)

The Meta Pixel allows us to track actions you take on our site (such as viewing a product, adding to cart, or completing a purchase) and link them to your Facebook or Instagram account. This helps us:

  • Show you relevant Cadomalo ads on Facebook and Instagram
  • Retarget visitors who viewed products but did not purchase
  • Build "lookalike audiences" of people similar to our existing customers
  • Measure the return on our ad spend

Governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

TikTok Pixel

The TikTok Pixel tracks events on our site (page views, product views, add-to-cart, purchases) so we can run and measure targeted ad campaigns on TikTok. It also enables retargeting — showing Cadomalo ads to people who have previously visited our site.

Governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Pinterest Tag

The Pinterest Tag tracks conversions (purchases) from our Pinterest ads and allows us to retarget visitors on Pinterest. It also helps us understand which Pinterest content drives the most sales.

Governed by Pinterest's Privacy Policy.

Google Ads

Google Ads cookies (including Google's conversion tracking tag) help us measure when a visitor clicks one of our Google ads and later makes a purchase. They also allow us to run remarketing campaigns — showing Cadomalo ads to previous visitors on Google Search, YouTube, and the Google Display Network.

Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How to Manage Your Cookie Preferences

4.1 Our Cookie Banner

When you first visit cadomalo.com, we display a cookie consent banner where you can choose to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or manage your preferences by category (Strictly Necessary, Analytics, Marketing). You can update your preferences at any time by clicking the cookie icon in the footer of our site.

4.2 Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete existing cookies
  • Block all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies specifically
  • Allow cookies from specific websites only

Here are links to cookie settings for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our site — for example, blocking all cookies may prevent your shopping cart from working correctly.

4.3 Opt-Out Links for Specific Platforms

  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): Manage ad preferences at facebook.com/ads/preferences
  • TikTok: Opt out in your TikTok account settings under "Ads"
  • Pinterest: Pinterest personalized ads settings
  • Google Ads: Google Ads Settings
  • Industry opt-out (US): aboutads.info and networkadvertising.org
