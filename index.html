Good to know

Questions parents ask

How do digital downloads work?

Right after checkout you get a download button on the confirmation page and an email with your files. Nothing is shipped — print at home or at any copy shop.

What ages are Cadomalo products for?

Each product shows its own age range: the picture books are for ages 3–7, the busy book, science kit, escape room and party kit for ages 5–7 (the party kit has tips for 3–4 and 8–10), and the personalised birthday story for ages 3–8.

Which languages are they in?

Every Human Body Wonders and Medical Heroes book has each page in English, French, Spanish and German. The activity kits and the personalised storybook are in English.

Can I print more than once?

Yes — as often as you need for your own family, or for one classroom. Sharing or reselling the files is not allowed.

What if something isn’t right?

Our 30-day happiness promise: write to support@cadomalo.com and we’ll fix it or refund you.
