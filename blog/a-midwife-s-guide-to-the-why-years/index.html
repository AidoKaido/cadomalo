A midwife’s guide to the “why?” years

From our midwife 1 min read 14 August 2026

Somewhere around age three, many children discover the most powerful word in the world: why. It can be wonderful and exhausting in the same afternoon.

It’s a good sign

“Why” means your child has noticed that things have causes. That’s the beginning of scientific thinking — and it deserves to be encouraged.

Turn the question around

“What do you think?” is a perfectly good answer. Children often have a theory already, and sharing it builds confidence.

“Let’s find out” is magic

You don’t need to know everything. Looking it up together in a book shows that not knowing is the start of learning, not a failure.

Keep a question jar

On busy days, write the question on a scrap of paper and put it in a jar. At the weekend, pick one and explore it properly. Nothing gets lost, and nobody feels brushed off.
