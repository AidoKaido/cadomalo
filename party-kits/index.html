Printable birthday party kits

A printable party kit gives you a whole birthday plan in one download: games, decorations, a story to tie it together and a schedule, so you are not inventing the party the night before. In the Birthday Adventure Party Kit, Twinkle the star invites the Birthday Captain and the Explorers to Wishland, where the Birthday Treasure Chest is locked. The children play their way through four adventure zones — the Detective Club, the Game Arena, the Creative Studio and the Treasure Quest — winning four treasure symbols, finding the magic word and unlocking the treasure together.

There are 16 games, most of which need no reading, plus a welcome poster, explorer passports, nine badges, clue cards, riddles and 14 photo booth props. You type the birthday child’s name and age straight into the PDF. A 90-minute schedule, a 60-minute “pick 6” plan, a supplies checklist, an answer key and six no-print backup games are included, and preparation takes about 30 minutes. It works for 4 to 10 children, is made for ages 5–7 with tips for ages 3–4 and 8–10, and suits every child. The pages are in English.

Questions parents ask

How much preparation does the party need?

About 30 minutes: print, cut and lay out the clues. The schedule tells you what happens when.

What ages and group size does it suit?

Made for ages 5 to 7, with tips for ages 3–4 and 8–10. It works for 4 to 10 children.

What else do I need?

A printer, scissors, glue, tape, crayons, a die (or the fold-up one in the kit), music, string, straws or sticks for the props and a few small favours for the treasure.
