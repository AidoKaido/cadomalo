Printable science discovery kits

Why does my heart beat faster when I dance? Where does my food go? Our discovery kits turn those questions into hands-on science your child can do at the kitchen table. My Amazing Body Discovery Lab follows Pico the little scientist and his buddies Thump, Noodle and Puff through five missions: super senses, the brilliant brain, heart and movement, bones and muscles, and the journey your food takes.

Each mission mixes a short explanation with a real experiment using everyday things — keys, a cup of water, string, straws, a zip-top bag, crackers, an old sock — then a puzzle, a chart or something to build. Your child collects stamps in a discovery passport, sets up a small home museum and earns a Junior Body Scientist certificate. Facts are written in kid-sized sentences and were checked against children’s health sources, which are listed on the grown-up pages. The kit has 38 pages in English for ages 5–7. Pair it with the ten Human Body Wonders books in the Ultimate Human Body Learning Pack: read the story, then try the experiment.

Questions parents ask

Do I need to buy science equipment?

No. The experiments use things most homes already have; everything is listed on page 3 of the kit.

Can a 4-year-old do it?

It is made for ages 5–7. Younger children can enjoy many of the missions with a grown-up reading aloud and helping with scissors.

Is it medical advice?

No. It is for learning and fun. The facts are simplified and checked against children’s health sources, but it does not replace advice from a health professional.
