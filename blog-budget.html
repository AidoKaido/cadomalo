5 Ways to Make a Budget Gift Land Big

  1. Personalize it. A $15 item with her name on it beats a $60 generic candle every time.
  2. Write a proper card. Not a sticky note — a real card, handwritten, with a specific memory or observation.
  3. Package it thoughtfully. Tissue paper, a small ribbon, a kraft paper bag. Total cost: $3. Perceived effort: massive.
  4. Pair two small items. Two $20 gifts presented together feel more generous than one $40 item.
  5. Deliver it personally if possible. The experience of receiving a gift in person, from someone who came specifically to give it, is the most underrated upgrade.
