The Ultimate Anniversary Gift Guide: By Year, Budget & Personality

Cadomalo Team·May 8, 2026·6 min read
Share:FacebookPinterest

Anniversary gifting has a unique pressure — you want to honor the time you've shared together without falling back on flowers and a card. This guide breaks it down by milestone year, budget, and personality so you always land with something meaningful.

By Milestone Year

1st Anniversary (Paper): Don't buy actual paper — give a personalized star map of the night you met, or a custom print of your first date location. Under $60 and infinitely more meaningful than a greeting card.

5th Anniversary (Wood): Our one of our personalized graphic tees is the perfect pick — personalized with your names and anniversary date, it's practical, lasting, and connects to the traditional "wood" theme beautifully. $79.99.

10th Anniversary (Tin/Aluminum): A a personalized print personalized with your favorite shared photos. Cozy, sentimental, and keeps giving every time they use it.

25th Anniversary (Silver): Our a personalized piece from our shop in silver plating — elegant, personal, and surprisingly affordable. Paired with a handwritten note about why you chose the piece, it becomes unforgettable.

50th Anniversary (Gold): The gold-plated a personalized piece from our shop paired with a framed a personalized print of your wedding night. This is the milestone gift — give it the effort it deserves.

By Budget

Under $30: our digital download — beautiful, instant, and paired with a heartfelt note it punches well above its price.

$30–$60: a personalized graphic tee or one of our quote shirts — used every day, a constant reminder of the person who gave it.

$60–$100: a personalized print (their wedding date, the city where you met), or the one of our personalized graphic tees.

Splurge-worthy: our top-selling personalized gift — three curated personalized items in a premium gift box. $99.99.

By Personality

Sentimental: a personalized print or a personalized photo item. Give them something that commemorates a specific moment in your relationship.

Practical: one of our personalized graphic tees or a personalized graphic tee — things they'll actually use, personalized so they never forget who gave it.

Romantic: a personalized piece from our shop in rose gold — wear-it-every-day jewelry that says "I chose you specifically."

Creative: one of our quote shirts with a line from your wedding song, a shared inside joke, or your first date song title.

3 Rules for Great Anniversary Gifts

  1. Reference a specific memory, not just a general sentiment. "Our first trip to Paris" beats "I love you."
  2. Presentation matters as much as the gift. A $40 item in a velvet box with a handwritten note lands harder than an $80 item in a plain bag.
  3. Timing is a gift too. A surprise delivery at work, or something waiting on the breakfast table, transforms the experience.
