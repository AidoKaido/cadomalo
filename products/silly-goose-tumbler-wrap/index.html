Your biggest questions, answered first.

Will it fit my tumbler?

The file is sized for 20oz skinny straight tumblers (9.3 × 8.2 in) and includes 16oz, 30oz and 2oz versions. Pick your size above — all sizes are in the download.

2

Will the print look blurry?

No. Every wrap is a 300 dpi PNG, drawn to be seamless, so there is no visible join where the ends meet.

No — this is a digital design file. You download it instantly and print it on sublimation paper or waterslide.
