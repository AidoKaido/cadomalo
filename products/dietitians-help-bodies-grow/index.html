Before you buy

Questions parents ask

Will it help my child feel less nervous about the doctor or dentist?

That is what the series is for. Each book shows one healthcare job in a calm, friendly way — what they do, the tools they use and how they help. Reading it before a visit gives your child words and pictures for what will happen.

What ages is it for?

Ages 3–7. Short sentences for little ones to repeat, and colouring pages older children can do on their own.

Four languages — won’t that confuse my child?

Children love spotting the same word in different languages. Every language sits in its own colour, so you read the one you speak at home and let the others become a game. Many families use it to keep a grandparent’s language alive.

How do I get it?

Right after checkout you get a download button on the confirmation page and an email with your PDF. Nothing is shipped — print at home or at any copy shop, as often as you need for your family.
