Questions parents ask

What age is it for?

Ages 5 to 7. Early readers can do a lot alone; younger children enjoy it with a grown-up reading aloud.

What do I need for the experiments?

Pencil, crayons, scissors, glue, tape and everyday things: keys, a cup of water, string, straws, a paper clip, a zip-top bag, crackers and an old sock. Everything is listed on page 3.

Is the science accurate?

Every fact uses short, kid-sized sentences and was checked against children’s health sources (KidsHealth by Nemours, Frontiers for Young Minds). The sources are listed on the grown-up pages. It is for learning and fun, not medical advice.

How do I get it?

Right after checkout you get a download button on the confirmation page and an email with your PDF. Nothing is shipped — print at home or at any copy shop, as often as you need for your family.
