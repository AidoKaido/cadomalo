Before you buy

Questions parents ask

Will my 3-year-old actually understand it?

Yes — that is the whole point. One idea per page, sentences a toddler can repeat, and friendly characters who explain things the way a child would. Older children can read along and colour the pages themselves.

Isn’t this just another printable?

It is a story first. Each book takes your child on a little adventure through one part of the body, then invites them to colour the pages they just read.

Four languages — won’t that confuse my child?

Children love spotting the same word in different languages. Every language sits in its own colour, so you read the one you speak at home and let the others become a game.

Is the science accurate?

Every book is written by a midwife and checked for accuracy and gentleness — simplified, never wrong.

How do I get it?

Right after checkout you get a download button on the confirmation page and an email with your PDF. Nothing is shipped — print at home or at any copy shop, as often as you need for your family.
