Questions parents ask

What age is it for?

Ages 5 to 7. The pieces are small, so it is not for children under 3, and a grown-up should do the cutting.

What else do I need?

A printer, card stock for the cut-out pages, a laminator or sheet protectors, about 80 pairs of Velcro dots and a dry-erase marker. None of these are included.

Will it really help with our routine?

Children cooperate more when they can see what comes next. Your child moves the picture cards themselves, so the routine becomes their job — and a game — instead of a list of instructions.

How do I get it?

Right after checkout you get a download button on the confirmation page and an email with your PDF. Nothing is shipped — print at home or at any copy shop, as often as you need for your family.

What if we don’t love it?

Our 30-day happiness promise: if a file doesn’t work or the product isn’t what you expected, write to support@cadomalo.com and we’ll make it right or refund you.
