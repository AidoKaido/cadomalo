Questions parents ask

What ages and group size?

Made for ages 5 to 7, with tips for ages 3–4 and 8–10. Works for 4 to 10 children, and it is gender-neutral.

How much preparation is it?

About 30 minutes: print, cut and lay out the clues. A 90-minute party schedule and a 60-minute “pick 6” plan are included.

What else do I need?

Printer, scissors, glue, tape, crayons, a die (or the fold-up one), music, string, straws or sticks for the props and a few small favours for the treasure.

How do I get it?

Right after checkout you get a download button on the confirmation page and an email with your PDF. Nothing is shipped — print at home or at any copy shop, as often as you need for your family.
