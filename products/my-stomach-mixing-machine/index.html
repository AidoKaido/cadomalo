Before you buy

Your biggest questions, answered first.

1

Will my 3-year-old actually understand it?

Yes — that is the whole point. One idea per page, sentences a toddler can repeat, and a friendly organ character who explains things the way a child would. Older children (6–8) get a “Did you know?” fact on every spread, so the book grows with them.

2

Isn’t this just another printable?

It is a story first. Each book follows a child and their new “body friend” from beginning to end, then invites your child to color the pages they just read. That is why kids ask for it again — and why they remember it.

3

Four languages — won’t that confuse my child?

Children love spotting the same word in different languages. Every language sits in its own color, so you read the one you speak at home and let the others become a game. Many families use it to keep a grandparent’s language alive.

4

Is the science accurate?

Every book is written by a midwife and checked for accuracy and gentleness — simplified, never wrong.

5

What if we don’t love it?

Our 30-day happiness promise: if the file doesn’t work or the book isn’t what you expected, write to us and we’ll make it right or refund you.
