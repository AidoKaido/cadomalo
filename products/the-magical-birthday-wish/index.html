Questions parents ask

How does the personalisation work?

After checkout you get a PDF with your Canva template links. Pick the hero look, type your child’s name once (Canva’s Find and replace changes it on every page), set the age and dedication, then download and print. Most parents finish in about 15 minutes.

Do I need a paid Canva account?

No — a free Canva account is all you need.
