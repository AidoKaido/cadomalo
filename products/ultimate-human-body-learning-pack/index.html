Questions parents ask

What ages is it for?

The books are written for ages 3–7. The Discovery Lab is made for ages 5–7; younger children enjoy it with a grown-up reading aloud.

Which languages are the files in?

Every Human Body Wonders book has each page in English, French, Spanish and German. The Discovery Lab pages are in English.

Is the science accurate?

The books are written by a midwife and checked for accuracy and gentleness. The Discovery Lab facts were checked against children’s health sources (KidsHealth by Nemours, Frontiers for Young Minds). It is for learning and fun, not medical advice.

How do I get it?

Right after checkout you get a download button on the confirmation page and an email with your PDF. Nothing is shipped — print at home or at any copy shop, as often as you need for your family.

What if we don’t love it?
