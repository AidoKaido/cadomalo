Picture books for curious kids

Cadomalo picture books answer the questions young children ask about their bodies and about the people who look after them. There are two collections of ten books each. Human Body Wonders gives one part of the body its own book — the brain, lungs, stomach, bones, muscles, skin, blood, eyes, ears and teeth — with friendly characters and one simple idea per page. Medical Heroes introduces ten healthcare jobs, from the doctor and the dentist to the paramedic and the dietitian, so that a check-up or a hospital visit feels familiar before it happens.

Every book is a 58-page square PDF: story pages first, then colouring pages of the scenes your child has just heard. Each page carries the text in English, French, Spanish and German, every language in its own colour, so you read the one you speak at home and the others become a game. The books are written for ages 3–7 by a midwife and checked for accuracy and gentleness. Buy a single title, a full collection of ten, or the Ultimate Human Body Learning Pack, which adds the Discovery Lab science kit to the ten Human Body Wonders books.

Questions parents ask

What ages are the books for?

Ages 3–7. Little ones listen and repeat the short sentences; older children read along and colour the pages on their own.

Do I have to read all four languages?

No. Every language has its own colour, so you simply read the one you speak at home. The other languages are there for curious children, bilingual families and grandparents.

Is this a printed book?

On this site the books are digital PDFs that you print at home or at a copy shop. A few titles are also sold as paperbacks on Amazon (English/French edition); that is a separate product from the four-language PDF.

Which should I choose: one book or a collection?

Start with the topic your child asks about most. A collection of ten costs $39.99 instead of $79.90 for the books one by one.
