Our Story

About Cadomalo

We started with a simple belief: every person deserves to feel genuinely seen on the moments that matter most.

Why We Exist

A gift is a message. We help you send the right one.

Cadomalo was founded on the belief that gifting should be personal, not generic. Too many people spend time and money on gifts that end up forgotten — not because they didn't care, but because they didn't have the right tools to express it.

We built Cadomalo to change that. Every product in our catalogue is designed to be personalized — to carry a name, a date, a memory, or a message that makes the recipient feel genuinely known and celebrated.

We're a proud US-based company, operating under Fokebo LLC, incorporated in Delaware. Our catalogue spans physical print-on-demand gifts, handcrafted personalized items, and instant-download digital products — all curated with care.

Our Values

What we stand for

Personal, Not Generic
Every product is designed to carry something personal — a name, a date, a place. Because the best gifts prove you were paying attention.
Quality You Can Trust
We personally review every supplier and print partner. If it doesn't meet our standards, it doesn't reach your doorstep.
Global Reach
We ship to 150+ countries. Whether you're buying for someone across the street or across an ocean, we've got you covered.
Your Happiness Guaranteed
30-day returns. No forms, no friction. If it isn't right, we make it right. That's our promise on every single order.
