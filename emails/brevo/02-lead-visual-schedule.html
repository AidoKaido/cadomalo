The 2-minute trick that ends morning battles

Hello again,

Most morning battles are not about the shoes or the toothbrush. They are about a small child being told what to do, again and again, by a hurried grown-up.

A visual schedule moves the “boss” job from you to a picture. Instead of “brush your teeth!”, you ask “what does the chart say comes next?” — and your child gets to be the one who knows.

Try this tomorrow (2 minutes tonight)

  • Lay out the routine pages in order before bed.
  • In the morning, point — do not repeat. Let your child say the next step out loud.
  • When a step is done, they tick it. When the chart is done, a high five. That is it.

It will not be perfect on day one — give it a few days of practice and see how it feels.
