A 5-minute senses game for tonight

Hello again,

Here is a game that needs nothing you have to buy, and that children ask to play again: the mystery bag. It turns the 5 senses from Mission 1 into a guessing game.

How to play

  • Fill a pillowcase (or a clean sock) with 3 or 4 safe objects your child knows: a spoon, a pine cone, a toy car, a soft scarf.
  • Touch only. Your child reaches in without looking and describes what they feel — smooth, bumpy, cold, squishy — before guessing.
  • Now smell. Eyes closed: half a lemon, a little cinnamon, a bar of soap. Which one is it?
  • Then listen. Shake a jar of rice, then a jar of coins. Can they hear the difference?
  • Swap roles. Your child fills the bag for you. They will love catching you out.

While you play, say what is happening: “your fingers sent a message to your brain, and your brain worked it out”. That one sentence is the big idea behind all five missions in the Discovery Lab.
