Cookie policy

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device. They help the site remember your basket and language, and — with your consent — measure how the site is used.

Cookies we use

Essential: basket, language, checkout security (always on). Analytics: anonymous visit statistics (only with consent). Marketing (only with consent): the Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) measures which ads lead to visits, sign-ups and purchases. With the same consent, our server also reports sign-ups and purchases to Meta through the Meta Conversions API, using your email address in hashed (scrambled, one-way) form, your IP address, browser type and the order value. If you decline marketing cookies, nothing is sent to Meta.

Your choice

You can change your choice at any time via the “Cookie settings” link in the footer, or block cookies in your browser settings.
